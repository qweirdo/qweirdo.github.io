This is art made slowly, and together. Colour gathers the way memory does, layer on layer, on aged manuscript vellum. Your cursor leaves currents. Your words leave weather. No one hand finishes a canvas.

  • SlowA canvas takes as long as it takes. 150,000 slow kinetic gestures. No skip, no undo, no rush.
  • KindGenerous words glow brighter in gold and lapis. Cruel terms are never repeated, only softened into quiet abstract geometry.
  • OpenFree to enter, free to leave. Not for profit, local-first privacy, with nothing sold.

How it works

  1. 01MoveEvery wandering gesture releases a thread of organic current tuned to the local biome.
  2. 02WritePlant up to five words. Waves, flames, starlight, roots, smoke, and ice awaken distinct glyphs.
  3. 03BloomLegible illuminated cartouches materialize with commanding presence, radiating micro-textures.
  4. 04ArchiveWhen 150,000 strokes gather, the finished edition is preserved in the archive. A fresh blank vellum begins.

About Qweirdo

Qweirdo is a not-for-profit generative art installation and digital humanities experiment in slow, collective authorship.

Topographic Micro-Textures. A single unified canvas houses distinct atmospheric biomes: fluid wave-currents in the abyssal south-west, crackling fire and floating embers in the caldera hearth, creeping root systems in the sacred understory, shimmering starlight across the astral zenith, fractured ice along the glacial ridge, and billowing atmospheric smoke through the ethereal mistlands.

Aged Manuscript Vellum. The canvas ground is an exquisite, tactile vintage parchment saturated with deep antique bronze, sepia ink, oxidized gold dust, organic mineral foxing, and medieval scribe marginal rulings.

Legible Glyphic Inscription. Words materialize not as lost noise, but as commanding, illuminated calligraphic emblems that anchor their local climate before dissolving into fluid abstraction.

Privacy & Ethos. Zero ads, zero tracking, zero analytics. Client-side local processing (`localStorage`). Code licensed under GNU AGPLv3; Prose & Artwork under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Privacy policy

What we collect: your name, an optional public link, and the date you agreed. No email, location, account or tracking cookies.

Why: only to credit you on the canvas edition you inspired. Words you plant are processed client-side in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Where it lives: credits show publicly on this page. Canvases and archive stay in your own browser storage.

Your rights: ask to correct or remove your name anytime via Project Qweirdo. Data is kept only while the project exists and is never sold or shared.

Children: please do not submit a name if you are under 13.
