About Qweirdo

Project Qweirdo is a proudly pointless digital sanctuary—and by virtue of that exact refusal, entirely singular. Unencumbered by feeds, algorithmic friction, telemetry, or modern web pollution, it offers a rare pause: a place to drift, doodle, and leave anonymous marks across a living, shared urban wall that breathes with time.

The Living Wall. Every stroke, tag, and geometric stamp merges into a real-time global fresco powered by edge vector streaming and contextual AI patina—keeping the wall alive across all devices without accounts or tracking.

Privacy Policy & Cultural Rights Charter

1. Zero Tracking: No analytics, cookies, fingerprinting, or ads. Your visit is completely untracked.

2. 100% Client-Side: All physics, rendering, and SHA-256 cryptographic hashes run locally in your browser.

3. Local Storage: Strokes, undo history, and download records stay in your browser's localStorage.

4. Public Offerings via GitHub: Submitting an offering to the wall edition is done publicly via GitHub Issues. Approved offerings are recorded in data/edition.json with words and moniker. Canvas rendering, local drafts, and credits stay strictly in your browser; nothing else is sent.

5. Contributor Credits: Submitting your name records only your moniker, optional link, and timestamp on the current edition.

6. License (CC BY-NC 4.0): Artworks are free to share, download, and teach non-commercially with attribution (https://qweirdo.github.io). Commercial and NFT use require prior agreement.

7. Data Erasure: Clear your browser data anytime to erase all locally stored records.

8. Children's Privacy: No personal data is collected from anyone under 13.
