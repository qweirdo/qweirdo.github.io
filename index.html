About Qweirdo

Project Qweirdo is a proudly pointless digital sanctuary—and by virtue of that exact refusal, entirely singular. Unencumbered by feeds, algorithmic friction, telemetry, or modern web pollution, it offers a rare pause: a place to drift, doodle, and leave anonymous marks across a living, shared urban wall that quietly breathes with time.

The Palimpsest Wall (Parchment & Street Canvas). An authentic mineral ink, aerosol, and street calligraphy installation set on a monumental paper-like, parchment-like yet wall-like canvas. Built with subtle laid-paper chain lines, watermark wire grids, natural mineral plaster craquelure, and deckled rag fibers. Visitors can freely sketch on the grand expanse or isolate focused mini-canvases in illuminated cartouches, blending them seamlessly back into the wall's perpetual stratigraphy with deckled paper teeth and gold leaf hairlines.

Real-Time Global Shared Wall (Serverless Commons). An open, decentralized commons connected via an ephemeral serverless pub/sub relay (ntfy.sh). Inscriptions from visitors worldwide stream live onto the master canvas with soft alpha blending and soft can-rattle chimes, preserving the last 50 pieces in an organic rolling archive without logins, user accounts, or tracking cookies.

Ambient Machine Conservator (Organic Breathing Weather). Rather than pushing automated content or artificial engagement loops, the machine conservator acts purely as slow weather and time. Lichen crystal blooms, soft patina washes, and mineral moisture runs settle softly around human-made tags, allowing the canvas to age like an authentic centuries-old urban wall without overpowering human marks.

Algorithmic Wildstyle & Scriptorium Palette. Revering underground subway typography and medieval illuminated manuscripts alike. Letterforms are fortified with 45-degree arrowheads, 3D extruded drop blocks, and power keylines, rendered through 10 museum-grade mineral pigments (Illuminated Gold, Lapis Lazuli, Venetian Oxblood, Verdigris Teal, Florentine Sage, Tyrian Purple, Walnut Bistre, Pompeian Terracotta, Sterling Silver, and Opal Obsidian) plus an infinite spectral color picker.

Philatelic & Geometric Stamp Forge. A contemplative imprint workshop inspired by classic philately (perforated postage teeth, circular cancellation postmarks, heraldic wax seals) and mathematical wonders (Metatron's cube, golden ratio nautilus spirals, Penrose decagrams, Guilloché banknote rosettes, flower of life). Visitors can emboss custom tag mottos and transfer them directly onto the monumental wall or active mini-canvases.

Cryptographic Provenance & Cultural Rights. Built for contemplation, creative play, and educational exploration. Every downloaded canvas is stamped with an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic hash key, exact UTC timestamp, and inspirer lineage. Absolute Privacy & Zero Telemetry. True digital sanctuary means zero ads, zero cookies, zero surveillance analytics, and zero telemetry. All fluid dynamics, aerosol spray mechanics, image composites, and cryptographic hashing execute locally in your own browser.

Privacy Policy & Cultural Rights Charter

1. Zero Surveillance, Zero Cookies, Zero Trackers: Project Qweirdo is an open, not-for-profit digital humanities installation. We do not use third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Meta pixels), no tracking cookies, no fingerprinting scripts, and no advertising networks. Your visit to this website is completely untracked.

2. 100% Client-Side Processing: All generative algorithms, fluid gesture dynamics, harmonograph calculations, stencil clipping, and cryptographic SHA-256 provenance hash generations (crypto.subtle) run entirely within your local browser sandbox. The strokes you paint, words you seed, and canvases you download remain on your device.

3. Local Browser Storage: Your stroke progress, canvas undo states, inspired contributor credits, and download validation records are saved strictly in your browser's localStorage (qw_s, qw_c, qw_a, qweirdo_download_records). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share or inscribe it.

4. Global Shared Wall Relay (Opt-In Public Broadcast): When you voluntarily click "Inscribe to Live Wall", your chosen moniker (defaulting to "Anonymous Scribe"), inscription title, inspirer lineage, and a compact WebP thumbnail are broadcast via a public serverless pub/sub relay (ntfy.sh/qweirdo_palimpsest_wall_v2) to appear on the live shared wall for other visitors worldwide. No IP addresses, device serials, or personal tracking identifiers are stored or retained by Project Qweirdo.

5. Voluntary Contributor Credits: When you submit your name to be credited as co-creator on the canvas edition, only the moniker, optional public link, and timestamp you provide are recorded to acknowledge your contribution.

6. Cultural Rights & Non-Commercial License (CC BY-NC 4.0): All artworks created on The Palimpsest Wall are free to download, study, teach, and share non-commercially with attribution to Project Qweirdo with URL: https://qweirdo.github.io. Any commercial exploitation, broadcast, or commercial NFT minting is strictly restricted and requires explicit prior agreement with the maintainer.

7. Your Rights & Data Erasure: You have full sovereignty over your data. You can clear your local cache, download records, and stored artworks at any time by clearing your browser's site data. If you have submitted a contributor moniker and wish to modify or remove it, contact the maintainer via Project Qweirdo.

8. Children's Privacy: This installation does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13.
